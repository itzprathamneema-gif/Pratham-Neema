# Gameplay Mechanics

## Racing Controls
- **Accelerate**: Press the 'W' key to speed up your vehicle.
- **Brake**: Use the 'S' key to slow down or stop.
- **Steering**: Move the mouse or use the arrow keys to control the direction of your vehicle.
- **Nitro Boost**: Activate the nitro boost with the 'N' key for temporary speed enhancements.

## Multiplayer Features
- **Online Racing**: Compete against players from around the world in real-time racing events.
- **Team Races**: Join or create teams to participate in team-based racing challenges and tournaments.
- **Voice Chat**: Communicate with teammates using built-in voice chat functionality during races.
- **Leaderboards**: Track your rankings against friends and global players on various leaderboards.

## Progression System
- **Levels and Experience**: Earn experience points (XP) by completing races, which allow you to level up and unlock new vehicles and upgrades.
- **Achievements**: Complete specific tasks to earn achievements and rewards.
- **Daily Challenges**: Engage in daily challenges for extra XP and unique rewards.

## Character Customization
- **Vehicle Skins**: Personalize your vehicle with a variety of skins and wraps.
- **Performance Upgrades**: Improve speed, handling, and durability by upgrading parts of your vehicle using in-game currency.
- **Avatar Customization**: Change your character's appearance, clothing, and accessories to stand out on the track.

## Monetization Details
- **In-Game Currency**: Players can earn or purchase in-game currency to buy upgrades and cosmetics.
- **Season Pass**: Opt for a season pass that grants access to exclusive content and rewards for a limited time.
- **Microtransactions**: Optional purchases for exclusive vehicles, skins, and customization options available in the store.
